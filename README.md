# 💡 Business Idea Generator Chatbot

An AI-powered Business Idea Generator that helps users discover suitable business ideas based on their **budget, skills, interests, and preferred business mode**.

The application combines a business idea dataset with **Generative AI** to provide personalized recommendations and business guidance through a conversational Streamlit interface.

## 🚀 Features

- 💬 Conversational chatbot interface
- 🔍 Business idea search and ranking
- 💰 Budget-based recommendations
- 🧠 AI-generated business responses
- 📊 Dataset-based business recommendations
- 🎯 Personalized suggestions based on user input
- 📋 Detailed business information
- 🌐 Interactive Streamlit web application

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Python**
- **Streamlit**
- **Pandas**
- **Generative AI / Gemini API**
- **python-dotenv**
- **Git & GitHub**
